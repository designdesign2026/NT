NewsTrackers is dedicated to collecting information about the wide range of issues impacting New Yorkers across the five boroughs and ensuring that members of the media have access to critical information to inform their work.

New York City is one of the most complicated, fast-paced, and important cities in the world. Every day, hundreds – if not thousands – of stories are unfolding. It is impossible to stay on top of all of them, and easy for important and impactful news to fall through the cracks.

Pedestrian Safety

NewsTrackers helps prevent that from happening. Right now, we are focused on the pedestrian safety crisis that is occurring across the city, collecting stories of individuals who have been injured by or had near-misses with irresponsible riders of bikes and/or e-bikes.
